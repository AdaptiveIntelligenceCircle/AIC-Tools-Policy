# Vietnam Risk Orientation Checklist

**Not legal advice. Not an official interpretation. Orientation only.**

## Context

- Component / scenario:
- Is there actual or planned use involving people or infrastructure in Vietnam?
- Data involved (if any):

## Questions

1. Deployment and users: where do operators and affected persons sit?
2. Personal data: is any personal data of individuals in Vietnam processed? Under what safeguards?
3. Sector: does the use touch regulated sectors (e.g., finance, health, transport, critical infrastructure)?
4. Nature of system: research reference vs. operational service with commitments?
5. Human oversight and fail-closed design: how are they realized?
6. Transparency: what can a reviewer inspect?
7. Local counsel: has qualified Vietnamese advice been obtained when impact is non-trivial?

## Outcome of this orientation pass

- [ ] Research / documentation only — continue with under-claim public language
- [ ] Needs deeper internal review
- [ ] Needs external qualified advice before any operational step
- [ ] Other (describe):

Remember: this checklist does not interpret Vietnamese law.
