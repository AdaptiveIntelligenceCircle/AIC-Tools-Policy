# EU AI Act — High-Level Orientation Checklist

**Not legal advice. Not an applicability determination. Orientation only.**

Use together with official texts and qualified counsel when real deployment or market claims are involved.

## Context

- Component / system name:

- Geographic reach of users / operators (if known):

## Questions (answer in prose; avoid yes/no only)

1. What is the system’s primary function in plain language?
2. Does it make or materially support decisions that affect people’s rights, safety, or access to essential services?
3. Is meaningful human oversight designed in for consequential outcomes?
4. Is the current status research / TestNet / experimental, or operational production?
5. What documentation and transparency artifacts already exist?
6. Are there prohibited-practice risk areas that need explicit exclusion by design?
7. What residual uncertainties remain?
