# Limitations

This document is part of the under-claim posture of AIC-Policy-Tools.

## 1. Not legal advice

Nothing in this repository is legal advice, a legal opinion, or a compliance determination.  
Qualified professionals and official authorities remain the proper sources for legal conclusions.

## 2. Heuristics are incomplete

Scripts and checklists use simplified dimensions.  
They omit many factors that matter in real regulatory analysis (sectoral rules, contractual context, cross-border transfers, enforcement practice, etc.).

## 3. Frameworks change

EU AI Act implementation, Vietnamese instruments, and other regimes evolve.  
Orientation notes can become outdated; always re-check primary sources.

## 4. No certification or badge

Passing a checklist or receiving a “low” heuristic band does not mean a system is safe, compliant, or approved.

## 5. Entity ≠ immunity

Use of these tools does not grant Adaptive Intelligence Circle, its maintainers, contributors, or any other party special legal status or immunity.

## 6. Pre-Covenant

Nothing here advances or constitutes a Covenant declaration or mainnet claim.

## 7. Jurisdiction and deployment matter

A research TestNet component and a production service with real users can fall under very different expectations even if the code is similar.  
Context is decisive; these tools only help structure questions about context.

## 8. Human judgment required

Every output is a prompt.  
Final classification, disclosure, and go/no-go decisions remain human responsibilities (and, where appropriate, professional ones).

---

When in doubt, treat any result from this repository as:

> “An orientation aid under the stated assumptions; not a determination.”
