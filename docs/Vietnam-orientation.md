# Vietnam-Related Orientation Notes

**Not legal advice. Not an official interpretation. Orientation only.**

## Purpose

Provide a lightweight orientation frame for contributors working with AIC materials who need to remain aware of Vietnam’s developing AI and digital-policy landscape.  
This note is deliberately high-level and under-claimed.

## Context (orientation)

Vietnam has been developing policy instruments related to artificial intelligence, digital infrastructure, and data.  
Instruments and guidance (including decrees, decisions, and strategies) should be read from official sources.  
Nothing in the AIC public repositories replaces those sources or the advice of qualified Vietnamese counsel.

## Orientation questions

1. What is the actual deployment location and user population of the component under discussion?
2. Does the component process personal data of individuals in Vietnam, and under what legal basis and safeguards?
3. Is the component offered as a research / open-source reference, or as a service with operational commitments?
4. Are there sector-specific rules (finance, health, transportation, critical infrastructure, etc.) that may apply independently of general AI policy?
5. How do transparency, human oversight, and fail-closed design choices interact with local expectations of accountability?

## AIC posture that remains constant

- Under-claim public language.
- Entity ≠ immunity.
- No token rule-power over protocol rules.
- Preference for Public Digital Infrastructure (PDI) framing where infrastructure is discussed.
- Pre-Covenant status until explicit checklist criteria are met.

## Relationship to risk classification

Use `checklists/vietnam-risk-orientation.md` and `checklists/risk-self-assessment.md` together.  
Escalate early when real users, real data, or real operational impact in Vietnam are involved.

For concrete questions, consult official texts and qualified professionals.
